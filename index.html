About Me

Full Stack Developer

I don't fit into a single box. I inhabit four.
Ethical hacking taught me to think like an adversary to find the crack before it's exploited, to distrust assumptions, to see what others overlook. I don't just scan for CVEs; I question logic flows, race conditions, and the quiet spaces between microservices where breaches take root.
Network engineering gave me a reverence for the invisible. Packets don't lie, latency reveals architecture flaws, and a misconfigured ACL can undo months of development. I build networks that breathe resilient, observable, and unforgiving to unauthorized hands.
Web development is where abstraction meets reality. I write code that doesn't embarrass itself under load. Accessible by default, performant by design and structured as if someone else will have to defend it, At 2am because eventually, someone will.
UI/UX Design is my bridge to humanity, Every interface I shape asks two questions: Does this feel inevitable? and Could this be weaponized? Usability without security is reckless. Security without usability is theater. I refuse both.

Most portfolios show what someone can do. Mine shows how I think systemically, skeptically, and with quiet obsession over the details most people scroll past. I don't promise perfection. I promise that I've already tried to break everything I built. And I'm still here.
